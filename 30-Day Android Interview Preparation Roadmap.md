# Daily Study Routine (4–5 Hours)

* **30 min** – Revise yesterday's topic
* **90 min** – Learn today's topic
* **90 min** – Write code and build a small example
* **45 min** – Solve interview questions for that topic
* **30 min** – Revise your notes and cheat sheet

## Most Important Advice

**Don't wait 30 days to start applying.**

Start applying from **Day 7 onward**. Companies usually take several days to schedule interviews. While applications are in progress, continue following this roadmap and revise before each interview.

For your preparation, I also recommend building an **"Android Interview Handbook"**. Every day, complete **one topic** using the master prompt you created (definition → 10 real examples → interview questions → code → revision notes). By the end of the month, you'll have a personalized handbook that you can revise quickly before any interview.
